Why United Hackathons

Built to connect innovators to organizers

Organizing a hackathon shouldn't mean starting from zero. We bring events together so every team inherits the knowledge, partners, and people that came before them.

Connect

Bring together organizers from different schools and regions into one collaborative ecosystem — with a direct line to people who have already run the event you're planning.

Support

Access sponsorship guidance, operational resources, mentorship, marketing, judging, and logistics support — the unglamorous work made dramatically easier.

Grow

Help more students discover hackathons while expanding each event's reach and impact — cross-promotion that turns local events into regional movements.

How it works

From application to a bigger hackathon

Four steps, no paperwork maze, no cost. Most teams are fully onboarded within two weeks.

  1. 1

    Apply to join

    Tell us about your event, your team, and what you're building toward. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

  2. 2

    Join the network

    Get onboarded into the organizer community and meet the teams running hackathons near you and around the world.

  3. 3

    Access resources

    Unlock shared documentation, sponsor introductions, judge and mentor pools, and partnership opportunities.

  4. 4

    Host together

    Run a larger, smoother, better-attended hackathon — then pass what you learned back to the next organizer.

FAQ

Questions, answered

United Hackathons is a student-led nonprofit that exists to connect innovators to organizers. We unite student-run hackathons into a single collaborative network where teams share resources, sponsors, mentors, and hard-won operational knowledge instead of rebuilding everything from scratch.

No. Membership is completely free. We're a nonprofit run by students — we never charge dues, and we never take a percentage of your sponsorship revenue or prize pool.

Once your event is accepted, it joins a shared ecosystem: sponsor and speaker introductions, cross-promotion to students in your region, access to pooled judges and mentors, and a living library of best-practice documentation maintained by organizers across the network. You stay fully independent — United Hackathons never takes over your brand or your event.
